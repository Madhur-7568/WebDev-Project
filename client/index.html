Architecture & Philosophy

How VAMS Powers Campus Events

Built upon the four pillars of flawless event coordination: Venue, Agenda, Management, and Seats.

V

Venue Coordination

Centralized directory of auditoriums, high-performance computing labs, and seminar halls with real-time calendar conflict resolution.

A

Agenda & Timelines

Automated timetable engine with JavaScript conflict detection that flags double-booked keynote speakers and room schedule overlaps.

M

Management System

End-to-end organizers suite: sponsor tracking, volunteer duty scheduling, itemized budget tracking, and real-time RSVPs.

S

Schedule & Seats

Interactive SVG visual seating matrix with VIP/Premium tiering, instant verified QR ticket passes, and turnstile check-in simulator.
